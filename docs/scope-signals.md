# Scope Signals

## Overview
Scope signals ensure that decisions or actions by AI systems stay within defined boundaries.

### Examples
1. **OutOfScopeWarning:** Triggers when an action is beyond defined system capabilities.
2. **ScopeBoundaryExceeded:** Indicates that operational guidelines have been violated.

### Importance
Scope signals prevent runaway scenarios and clarify AI capabilities to users and operators.

### Implementation
All scope signals rely on clear definitions, boundaries, and compliance frameworks to guide AI actions effectively.
