# Human-in-the-Loop

## Overview
Human-in-the-loop systems ensure AI workflows stay accountable, safe, and ethical where judgment or responsibility is present.

## Principles
1. Employ human review for tasks involving judgment or critical accountability.
2. Ensure traceability and transparency for all significant AI decisions.

## Implementation
AI systems interacting with this protocol must:
- Route uncertain decisions via clear signals.
- Follow boundary signals ensuring appropriate human intervention.
